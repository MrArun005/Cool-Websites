# Owner's head meshes

`head.stl`, `head_bot_sheet.stl`, `antenna.stl`, `left_antenna_holder.stl`, `right_antenna_holder.stl`
are copied unchanged from `duckbody/model/upstream/assets/` (Open Duck Mini v2, Apache-2.0, see `LICENSE`
and `duckbody/model/UPSTREAM.md`). They are the head parts of the owner's v3 duck
(`duckbody/v3/duck_mesh_v3_head60_jaw22.glb` is these parts plus the body, fused, scaled 0.7).
The hybrid uses them at the same 0.7 scale as `duckbody/model/duck_mid.xml`; the bill (upper bill
and hinged lower jaw) is the owner's, from `duck_mid.xml` (`duckbody/build_mid.py`).
