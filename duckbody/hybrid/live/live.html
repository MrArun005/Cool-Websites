    What runs here, and what is simplified

    Physics
    MuJoCo 3.15 compiled to WebAssembly (Google DeepMind's official build), 5 ms steps, contacts between ducks on. The same model as the Python match: Microduck's body with every part colliding, your head, a 4.5 × 3 m pitch (RoboCup KidSize × 0.5), goals with nets, a 7 cm 20 g ball.
    Servos
    BAM's XL330 model on all 154 servos, ported to JavaScript: firmware P loop, DC motor with back-EMF, load-dependent friction, battery sag per duck, 15–30 ms command delay. Every duck gets a random body (mass, grip, servo strength, battery) like Microduck's training.
    Brains
    Microduck's ONNX files, unchanged: velstand walks, turns and gets up after a fall; ball_kick_left and ball_kick_right kick; roulade frees a stuck duck. They run from the ONNX weights in plain JavaScript (checked against onnxruntime to 0.000006).
    Tactics
    The match code's team tactics, ported line by line (same commands on 60 000 recorded decisions): a striker and a second player on the ball, support runs, marking, passes, a goalkeeper that comes out.
    Simplified
    The head cameras are not rendered. A player sees the ball when it is inside its camera's real field of view, not hidden behind another duck and close enough to show as a blob; the position it gets has noise that grows with distance. Self-localization is the true pose plus a drifting error, as in the Python match. On a slow computer the match runs in slow motion rather than skipping physics; the badge on the pitch says how slow.
