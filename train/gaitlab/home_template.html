Bolt at home

Bolt lives in this room as a pet. It gets tired, playful, curious and lonely, and it picks what to do on its own: sniffing round the room, batting its ball about, racing round with the zoomies, napping on its bed, begging for a fuss, or sulking with its back to you if you ignore it too long. Talk to it (type, or use the mic), throw its ball, praise it or tell it off, and it remembers you. Every move is Bolt's own trained brains in real physics, running in your browser: nothing about its body is animated by hand.
