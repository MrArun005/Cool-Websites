Bolt Agility Cup

A dog-agility competition. The judge sets out a numbered course, and four of Bolt's trained brains run it one after another with the same handler. Weave through every pole, take each tunnel from the right end, and lie on the pause table for the judge's count of five. The judge counts 5 faults for a missed pole or a wrong tunnel entry, and 1 fault per second over the standard course time. The Championship class is much harder: 12 tighter poles, three tunnels, sharper turns and a strict course time, so even the best dogs rarely go clear. Hurdles come back when Bolt has learned to jump. The fewest faults wins, then the fastest time. A fall is elimination. Everything runs in your browser, in the same physics as training.
