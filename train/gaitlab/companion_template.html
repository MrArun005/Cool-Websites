      Local language model (advanced, off)

      If you run an open-weight model on this computer (for example a llama.cpp server, or any OpenAI-compatible server), Bolt can use it with the same personality and safety rules.       What is real here (simulation)

      • The body is physics. MuJoCo runs in this page with the motor limits used in training. Bolt's trained brains move every leg 50 times a simulated second. Learned (the brains do it all): stand, sit, lie down, get up, walk, come closer, turns, spin (the sharp-turn walk brain, a full circle) and zoomies (trot out, walk back). If Bolt falls, its self-righting brain gets it up.
      • Assisted (the posture brain holds the legs; a guide in the physics makes the move, as in Bolt Dash): hop and happy bounce (an upward kick on the body plus a little lift in the air), wiggle (a twist torque), play bow (a nose-down torque, about 25°), twirl (a hop with a twist). The brains never learned these.
      • Drawn only (no physics): head tilt, nod, ears, tail wag, the eyes and the mood lights. The simulated dog has no head, ear or tail joints.
      • The mind is rules, written by hand, running in this page: it reads your words (feelings with negation and intensity, sarcasm cues, emoji, punctuation, the time of day), plans a tone, and picks lines, jokes and moves. Its guess about how you feel can be wrong; tap “Not quite” to correct it. It is not a language model and it does not understand everything. It is a robot, not a therapist.
      • Dance: Bolt's tune has known beats; for your music the beats are found in this page from the decoded file; the microphone mode follows onsets live. Moves start on the beat; the physics adds at most one control step (20 ms).

      Privacy

      • Everything Bolt hears, reads and remembers stays on this device. Memory (your name, a few things you share, recent chat, which jokes you liked) is kept in this browser's storage; untick “Remember things” or press “Forget everything” at any time.
      • Voice: speaking uses voices on your device unless you allow online voices. Voice input is off by default: many browsers send speech recognition audio to the browser maker's servers (Chrome sends it to Google). Bolt uses on-device recognition where the browser offers it and tells you which one you have.
      • Music and the microphone for dancing are analysed in memory, live, and never stored or sent. No camera is used.
