Bolt's Day Out

Nothing here is a recording. The physics runs in your browser, Bolt's trained brains choose every motor command fifty times a second, and a small dog mind decides what to do next from how tired, curious and playful Bolt feels, plus a roll of the dice. It sniffs the bins, trots about, gets the zoomies, naps by the porch, takes the jumps and stops at the kerb. Every run is different. Call it, throw its ball, give it a shove or set up a jump.
