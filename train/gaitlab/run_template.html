Bolt Run

The dog-catcher is after Bolt. Steer between the crates, bins and posts, jump the hurdles, and keep ahead. Bolt is not animated: it runs live in this browser with MuJoCo physics and its trained brains choosing every motor command fifty times a second, so it can stumble, knock a bar or fall, and it has to get itself up.

What is real here

  • Bolt's running is the physics. MuJoCo with the Unitree A1 motor model, as in training. The trot, walk, canter and turns come from the dog-motion brains (imitating a real dog's motion capture), sharp turns from the walk brain, jumps from the jump brain, getting up from the self-righting brain. Nothing moves Bolt but its motors.
  • You choose, Bolt does. Left and right pick a lane; Bolt's brains decide how to get there. A trotting Bolt cannot steer (those brains learned a straight trot), so to change lane it slows to a walk, turns, lines up and trots on again. That is why a lane change costs time.
  • Real things to hit. Crates, bins, posts and the walls are solid bodies in the physics; touching them is a real contact, and it can trip Bolt. Hurdles are real agility hurdles: the bar sits loose in its cups and falls if a paw hits it. A jump scores only if the bar stays up, every paw crosses it and Bolt does not fall.
  • Jumping. Up arms the jump when a bar is 1.2–4.5 m ahead and Bolt is lined up on it; at 1.1 m the jump brain takes over. It needs a trot: from a walk Bolt runs out.
  • What is made up. The dog-catcher is drawn only: a figure with a speed that rises with the distance (0.75 m/s plus 0.3 m/s per 100 m, at most 1.6 m/s), never more than 9 m behind, and 2 m nearer each time Bolt runs into something. It has no body in the physics and never touches Bolt; it catches Bolt when it gets within 1 m.

Headless checks (train/gaitlab/run_test.mjs, simulation, 30 Oct). An autoplayer that sees the rows ahead, 8 runs of up to 2 minutes: 48–115 m (mean 82), caught in 7 of 8 after 49–114 s; 6 jumps, 4 clean, 3 falls (all at jumps, all got up alone), 15 run-outs (Bolt drifted off line before the bar), 5 hurdles run through, 8 bumps. A do-nothing player, 4 runs: caught every time, after 53–64 s and 50–62 m. Bolt's mean speed is about 0.95 m/s: a trot is 1.7–1.8 m/s, but every lane change or drift correction is done at a walk.
