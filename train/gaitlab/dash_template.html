What is real here (simulation)

  • Bolt's body and legs are physics. MuJoCo runs in this page, with the motor limits used in training. Every leg motor command comes from Bolt's trained dog-motion brains, 50 times a simulated second: the trot, then the canter brain once the pace is high. Feet push, slip and land for real, and if Bolt falls, its self-righting brain gets it up.
  • Steering and the hop are assisted. Those brains learned to run straight, not to dodge. So the lane you pick does not go through them: a guide force on Bolt's body pulls it onto the lane, a torque holds its heading and keeps it level, a forward force holds the run speed, and a hop is an upward kick with some lift while in the air. The brains do not steer here.
  • Obstacles are solid. Crates, barrels, barriers and bars are free bodies with real mass; hit one and it goes flying and Bolt stumbles. A bar drops at the slightest touch. Treats and the dog-catcher are drawn only (no bodies in the physics); the catcher closes in whenever Bolt is slower than the pace (a crash costs 1.8 m, a knocked bar 1 m).
  • The pace rises. The speed asked goes from 1.7 to 2.7 m/s over the first 320 m, and the page runs the simulated clock up to 1.35 times real time.

Headless checks (train/gaitlab/dash_test.mjs, simulation, 30 Oct; real runs through this page's code, pressing only left, right, hop and drop). An autoplayer that sees the rows ahead, 6 runs of 5 minutes: never caught, 640–644 m each, 1–3 crashes, 33–37 of 39–42 bars cleared, 0 falls. A human-like player (reacts 0.25 s after something comes into view, hops a little early or late, now and then late or in the wrong lane), 6 runs: caught after 117–161 s at 211–312 m, 2–6 crashes. A player who presses nothing: caught after 13–24 s. Key to visible motion: a lane change moves Bolt 5 cm in 60 ms of simulated time and puts it in the new lane in 340 ms; a hop lifts it 3 cm in 20 ms. One control step (brains, guide and 10 physics steps) costs about 2.3 ms in Chromium, about 3.3 ms per 60 Hz frame at the fastest clock.
