Bolt's Castle

Like Takeshi's Castle: every game builds a new course, and Bolt has to get through it. Sprint, weave through the poles, jump three bars without touching them, cross the narrow bridge without falling in the water, sit in the box, lie on the mat and reach the castle. The guard shouts orders at any moment, and so can you. Bolt really jumps: it stops in front of each bar, steadies itself and leaps, with a jump brain it learned by trial and error. Five levels, each harder: higher bars (12 cm up to 22 cm), tighter time limits, a narrower bridge, more poles, more shouts with less time to obey, and from level 3 an ice rink and the guard's cannon, which shoves Bolt sideways. Bolt has 3 lives: a failed station or a fall costs one. Everything that stands up is solid: the hurdles, the slalom posts, the finish posts and the castle's towers and walls. Bolt plays it with its own trained brains, in the same physics as in training, running in your browser.
