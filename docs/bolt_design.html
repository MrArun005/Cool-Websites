Bolt

The robot dog every Bolt brain is trained on: a 12 kg quadruped with three motors per leg, front knees bending back and rear knees bending forward. Taken from the simulation model train/bolt.xml, unchanged since 28 October.

Side view, to scale

Knees > < Seen from the side with the head on the right, the rear knee points forward and the front knee points back, so the two knees point toward each other, like a real dog's leg joints. The Unitree Go2 bends all four knees backward instead. Drawn in the standing pose the brains use: hip 15.7°, knee 31.5°.
