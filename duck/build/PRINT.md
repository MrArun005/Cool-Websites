# Bolt Duck: what to print

**51 pieces from 36 designs.** Files: the `print/` folder of
[Open Duck Mini v2](https://github.com/apirrone/Open_Duck_Mini/tree/main/print), or run
`./get_parts.sh` to download exactly the version this plan was checked against.

- **Material:** standard PLA at 15% infill. Only `foot_bottom_tpu.stl` is TPU at 40%.
- **Printer size:** the head is **200 × 197 mm**, so you need a bed of at least 200 × 200 mm
  (Ender-3, Prusa MK4, Bambu A1/P1S/X1 all fit; the A1 mini does not). Every other part is 153 mm or smaller.
- **Plastic:** about 0.5 kg according to the project, up to ~1 kg by our volume estimate: buy a 1 kg spool.
- **No printer?** Libraries and makerspaces often print for the cost of filament; online services
  quote per part. Send them this list.

Sizes measured from the STL files. Print the small leg parts first to check your servo fit.
